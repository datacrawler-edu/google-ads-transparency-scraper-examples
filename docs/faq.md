# FAQ

## What can I search for?

Use a website domain or URL, advertiser name, or advertiser ID.

## Why can a run return no rows?

The selected advertiser, filters, dates, or platform may have no matching public creatives. Try a broader date range or fewer filters.

## What does enrichment add?

With `includeDetails`, the Actor attempts to read observable preview text and asset URLs from public creative previews. It charges the enrichment event only when usable enrichment data is returned.

## Is the data private?

The Actor is intended for public Google Ads Transparency Center records. Follow applicable law and the source site's terms.
